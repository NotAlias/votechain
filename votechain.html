No ticket yet? Make your own voter key

Use this only if the organiser asked voters to register their own keys before the election was created. Send the public key to the organiser. Keep the ticket secret: it is what lets you vote.

Check my vote

Paste your ticket, your public key, a ballot ID or your receipt. Everything is checked locally against the chain file you loaded.

Call a new election

Every voter belongs to exactly one pool. Binding pools decide the official result; advisory pools are counted and published separately. Either let this page generate tickets for you to hand out, or paste public keys that voters generated themselves.

Close the election and publish the count

Load and merge every chain file you have received first (Chain file tab). Closing releases the ballot decryption key into the final block so anyone can re-run the count. This cannot be undone.
